# Wilson family ranch: Sapaque / Waller Creek: history notes for the photo-history site

**Pin:** 35.8426537, -121.1295293 (WGS84). Section 17, T24S R8E, Mount Diablo Meridian (township from BLM CadNSDI PLSS service; the section number is printed at the pin on every USGS topo 1919-1995). The pin is in Monterey County on the Burnett Peak 7.5' quad, on the north side of the Waller Creek drainage, just south-east of a 1,202 ft knob.
**Fort boundary:** the pin is about 150 m outside the present Fort Hunter Liggett boundary (US Census TIGERweb "Military Installations" layer).

> Ownership rule used here: a name appears only if a cited source supports it. Everything else says **unknown**.

| 1834 onward | Secularization: mission lands split into at least 10 Mexican grants. | Wikipedia rancho articles |
| 1839-1845 | Nearby grants: **San Miguelito de Trinidad** (provisional 1839, granted 1841 to José Rafael Gonzales, 22,136 ac); **El Piojo** (1842, Joaquín Soto, 13,329 ac, along Piojo Creek); **Los Ojitos** (1842, Mariano de Jesús Soberanes, 8,900 ac, now mostly under Lake San Antonio); **Pleyto** (1845, Antonio Chaves); **Milpitas** (the Jolon-valley rancho Hearst later bought). ## 2. Per-photo blurbs (match `manifest.json` order)

1. **1919 - USGS Bryson 15' topo (surveyed 1917).** Owner: US public land. Lewis H. Smith's homestead claim was probably pending: he received the adjacent SE1/4 in 1920 and the pin's SW1/4 in 1926 (pending claim is an inference).
   - Waller Creek and its South Fork are already named.
   - "Quicksilver Mine" is printed on Sycamore Creek about 1.4 km NW of the pin; this is today's Botts mine.
   - The Bryson post office (opened 1887, once called Sapaque) was ~5 km SE.
2. **1949 - USGS Burnett Peak / Bryson 7.5' (from 1947 photos).** Owner: after Lewis H. Smith's 1926 patent; later owners unknown.
   - The new Hunter Liggett Military Reservation (1941) boundary is drawn right beside the pin.
   - No roads or buildings are mapped at the pin.
   - The Sam Jones fire would sweep the area four years later (July 1953).
3. **1961 - USGS Bryson 15' (from 1960 photos).** Owner: after Lewis H. Smith's 1926 patent; later owners unknown.
   - Beartrap Road, Bald Mountain (2,132 ft, 2.3 km N) and Smith Ranch are shown.
   - The Bryson PO had closed in 1937.
   - The 1953 Sam Jones fire (35,455 ac) had burned over this ground.
4. **1973-04-21 - Landsat 1 MSS (false color).** Owner: after Lewis H. Smith's 1926 patent; later owners unknown.
   - This is from the first civilian Earth-observation satellite program (launched 1972).
   - 60 m pixels: red is green vegetation.
   - Lake San Antonio (dam built 1960s) shows dark to the east, over the old Los Ojitos rancho.
5. **1979 - USGS 7.5' photo-revised (1976 photos).** Owner: c.1976+ Wilson family (per James).
   - The family's first years.
   - The map was updated from 1976 aerial photos; purple tint marks revisions on the Bryson sheet.
   - Still no structures mapped at the pin.
6. **1984-05-27 - Landsat 5 TM.** Owner: Wilson family.
   - Landsat 5 ran 1984-2013, a record for an Earth-imaging satellite.
   - It shows spring-green hills across the whole Waller Creek / Nacimiento area.
7. **1994-05-11 - NAPP aerial (UCSB FrameFinder).** Owner: Wilson family.
   - First aerial photo of the homesite: a clearing with a loop drive, and a dirt road along the creek.
   - Two ponds hold water ~0.8 km SSW.
   - Botts mine workings lie 1.4 km NW, inside the fort.
8. **1995 - USGS Burnett Peak 7.5' (1994 photos).** Owner: Wilson family.
   - Dirt roads are now mapped at the pin.
   - Fort Hunter Liggett's boundary is printed as a red band just N/W.
   - This is the last paper edition of this quad.
9. **1996-05-12 - Landsat 5 TM.** Owner: Wilson family.
   - Regional spring view.
   - Fort Hunter Liggett's open training lands lie west and north.
10. **2002-05-31 - NAPP color-infrared aerial.** Owner: Wilson family.
    - Infrared film shows healthy oaks and chaparral as red.
    - Layout is unchanged from 1994.
11. **2005-06-06 - Landsat 5 TM.** Owner: Wilson family.
    - Early-summer regional view.
    - Grass is starting to cure.
12. **2010-05-08 - Microsoft UltraCam aerial (Esri Wayback).** Owner: Wilson family.
    - 0.3 m detail.
    - Two tracks climb the slope NE of the house.
13. **2012-05-18/20 - USDA NAIP 1 m.** Owner: Wilson family.
    - Roofs are visible at the pin and at a neighbouring clearing ~250 m W.
14. **2016-06-25 - NAIP 0.6 m.** Owner: Wilson family.
    - A new graded area opens north of the house.
    - The Chimney fire (Aug 2016, 46,234 ac) burned within ~3 km to the south-east a few weeks later.
15. **2020-07-04 - NAIP 0.6 m.** Owner: Wilson family.
    - The graded area keeps growing.
    - The tiny Copperhead fire (Oct 2020) was ~2.6 km away.
16. **2024-05-26 - NAIP 0.6 m.** Owner: Wilson family.
    - Largest cleared area so far, with new tracks and pads.
17. **2025-07-08 - Vantor Legion-02 satellite (Esri World Imagery).** Owner: Wilson family.
    - 0.34 m commercial satellite view.
    - Summer-gold grass.
18. **2026-11-11 - Sentinel-2B.** Owner: Wilson family.
    - The freshest clear satellite pass (one week before this was compiled).
    - ESA Copernicus data, free worldwide.
