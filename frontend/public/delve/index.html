Delve is original code written for DXEdge. The walls, sprites, runes, and this page's favicon are drawn in the browser. The favicon is the DXEdge mark in an ember tint. Sound is synthesized here. There are no ads and no third-party trackers. It is a grid dungeon in the spirit of late-1980s first-person crawlers, and it does not use their art, names, or code.

controls

Help

  • W or ↑ forward. S or ↓ back.
  • A or ← turn left. D or → turn right.
  • Q strafe left. E strafe right. The pad under the view does the same by mouse or touch.
  • Space swings every living champion in the front rank, and also the selected champion if they stand in the back.
  • Z Bryn, X Cara, C Ivo, V Senn. Each attacks alone. Shift+1 through Shift+4 do the same.
  • F or Enter uses a door, a chest, a lever, or the stairs. Click a champion to select them. Click a vial to drink it.
  • 1 to 5 speak Kael, Sera, Veth, Orin, and Nim. Tab cycles the selected champion.
  • H or ? opens this list. M mutes the hum and the effects. The volume slider is in the header. Esc pauses.

Seven floors. A warden holds the fifth stair. The seal at the bottom is the last fight.
