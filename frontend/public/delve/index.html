Credits

Delve is original code written for DXEdge. The walls, sprites, portraits, particles, runes, and this page's favicon are drawn in the browser. The favicon is the DXEdge mark in an ember tint. Sound is synthesized here. There are no ads and no third-party trackers. It is a grid dungeon in the spirit of late-1980s first-person crawlers, and it does not use their art, names, or code.

controls

Help

  • W or ↑ forward. S or ↓ back.
  • A or ← turn left. D or → turn right.
  • Q strafe left. E strafe right. The pad under the view does the same by mouse or touch.
  • Space swings every living champion in the front rank, and also the selected champion if they stand in the back.
  • Z Bryn, X Cara, C Ivo, V Senn. Each attacks alone. Shift+1 through Shift+4 do the same.
  • F or Enter uses a door, a chest, a lever, or the stairs in the next tile, and talks to someone standing there. If it is further down the hall, the log names it once. Click a champion's portrait to open their character sheet. Click a vial to drink it.
  • J opens the quest log. The story runs down the seven floors. Side work is marked on the map: a lost adventurer, a shard to bring back, a monster nest, and a cracked niche.
  • The stair down stops at the waystation, a safe camp between floors. The campfire, Sable, and Nell are ahead of the near stair. Talk with F, rest at the campfire, then take the far stair to go deeper. The near stair returns you to the floor you left. The map zooms to the camp. B opens Nell's stall. Coins buy a hard biscuit, a waterskin, a red vial, or a carried torch.
  • What you face is named in the log. Stairs are a dark well with gold steps. A chest, a lever, a locked door, an iron gate, a key, a vial, a wall torch, a tattered banner, scattered bones, a cobweb, a stone figure, the campfire, a floor plate, a cracked niche, and a person each say what they are, and each one is drawn in the hall. Press F to use the one in front of you. A wall torch can be carried. Step on a floor plate to lift its gate. The campfire lets the party rest, and for 12 coins it lifts a champion who has fallen. The same line stacks, as in x4, instead of filling the log. A step count is said once until you move.
  • 1 2 3 4 open Bryn, Cara, Ivo, and Senn. I opens the selected champion. The character sheet shows stats, skills, a paperdoll, and a backpack. Slots are head, body, legs, feet, hands, neck, weapon, and offhand. Drag gear between those slots, the backpack, and the other champions. Drop it on the floor. Double-click to equip, eat, or drink. G swaps the front rank and the back. The front rank takes the melee. The back rank reaches farther. The delve pauses while the sheet is open. Esc closes it.
  • Click a rune, or hold Alt and press 1 through 9. 1 2 3 are Spark, Brand, Crown. 4 5 6 are Ash, Vita, Glim. 7 8 9 are Bolt, Mend, Shell. R casts the three. Spark + Glim + Bolt is light. Brand + Ash + Bolt is a fireball. Spark + Vita + Mend heals. Spark + Glim + Shell is a shield. Mana is on the rune. Casting skill rises as you use it. Tab cycles the selected champion.
  • Three save slots live in this browser. Reaching the waystation writes an autosave. Load any of them from the title.
  • A crypt ghost ignores steel. Magic hurts it, and a closed door stops it. Other creatures hear you, chase, flee when hurt, and strike harder in a pack. Some will force a door.
  • H or ? opens this list. M mutes the hum and the effects. The volume slider is in the header. Esc closes this list, the quest log, or a character sheet, and otherwise pauses.
  • Graphics (low, medium, high) and movement (smooth or step) sit on the title card and in the header. Low skips the extra torch lights. The lantern flicker stays slow on every setting.

Seven floors, each with its own stone: crypt, caves, flooded halls, and a lava forge. A warden holds the fifth stair. The seal at the bottom is the last fight.
