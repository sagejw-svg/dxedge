IRIS

Iris plays whatever the camera sees. A line sweeps the picture and turns each row's brightness into a pitch. Hue picks a chord, motion fires notes where the frame changes, and edges stir a noise texture. The picture stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How to play

  1. Allow the camera, or press Turn on camera if the browser asks for a gesture. Prefer a still? Choose Use a picture. The file is read locally.
  2. Scanline sweeps left to right. Bright rows sing higher. Speed is how fast the line moves. Snap to scale and the root key lock the pitches.
  3. Color listens to the whole frame. The dominant hue chooses the chord, saturation opens the filter, and overall brightness is the volume.
  4. Motion is quiet until something moves. Each cell of the grid is a pitch, and a hit marks the attack. Wave, or walk through the frame.
  5. Edges turns contrast into a grainy texture. A busy picture is thick. A flat wall is nearly silent.
  6. Freeze holds the current frame. Rec writes a WAV of the master output. S M L XL changes how big the panel is. Reverb and Volume sit on the master.
