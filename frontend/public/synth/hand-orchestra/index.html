How to play

Right hand, or the mouse keys marked Play: each raised finger is one voice of the chord. Thumb is the root an octave down, index the root, middle the 3rd, ring the 5th, pinky the 7th. Curl a finger and that voice stops.

Lift the play hand for a higher octave. The slider does the same thing with the mouse. Keys 1–5 toggle the play fingers. Arrow up and down move the octave.

Left hand, or the Chord buttons: count fingers.

Camera, calibration, smoothing

The camera starts when the page opens. The picture is mirrored so it feels like a mirror, and the dots sit on the fingertips. If you turn the camera off, it stays off until you press Turn on camera. MediaPipe reads the frames here. They are not uploaded and there is no account.

Controller

The same gestures leave as MIDI notes and control changes, and they can play a synth in the DXEdge rack. Pinch, height, depth and tilt each get a CC number and a knob.

Web MIDI is off until you open an output. Safari has no Web MIDI. The built-in orchestra still plays.

Rack

The rack stays quiet until you drive it. Saved patches on this browser are put back after each gesture.

Finger tracking uses MediaPipe Hand Landmarker by Google, Apache License 2.0. The model is downloaded into this browser the first time you use the camera. Camera frames are processed here and are not uploaded. Part of the Filament rack on DXEdge.
