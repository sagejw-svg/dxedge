HEXODE

Six-operator FM. Computer keys play it, a MIDI keyboard plays it, and a .syx bank you load replaces the init voice.

Keys: the letter row is one octave from C, the row below is the octave under it. Space or Panic stops every note. Hold, or the ` key, keeps notes sounding. Knobs and sliders drag up and down; hold Shift for a finer drag, and use the arrows or the mouse wheel while a control is focused. S M L XL is remembered for the whole site.

Hexode uses the FM engine by Matt Montag, MIT license, commit f269f0e. A factory cartridge is not included. Load a 32-voice .syx of your own. Part of the Filament rack on DXEdge.
