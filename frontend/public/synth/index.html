FILAMENT

A tube synthesizer. The tubes do real work: one drives the filters, one sits inside the Valve filter's resonance loop, and a push-pull pair runs the output. Play it with the keys below, your computer keyboard, or a MIDI keyboard.

Playing it. Click or touch the keys (lower on a key is louder). Computer keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play an octave, Z X shift the octave, C V set velocity, space is the sustain pedal. Knobs: drag up or down (shift for fine), arrow keys, double click to reset. A MIDI keyboard works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox after you press Connect MIDI. Safari has no Web MIDI, so on an iPhone or a Mac in Safari use the keys on screen.
