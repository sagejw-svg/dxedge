FILAMENT

Cathode, the second device in the rack: one voice, two VCOs, a screaming high-pass and low-pass pair, and a patch panel. Everything is pre-wired; a cable overrides the normal printed under a jack.

A tube synthesizer. The tubes do real work: one drives the filters, one sits inside the Valve filter's resonance loop, and a push-pull pair runs the output. Play it with the keys below, your computer keyboard, or a MIDI keyboard.

Dekatron, a counting-tube drum machine: kick, snare, hats, clap, tom and rim, synthesized, with a 16-step pattern. The keys play the voices. Swing, accents and the pattern length live on the panel.

Playing it. Click or touch the keys (lower on a key is louder). Computer keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play an octave, Z X shift the octave, C V set velocity, space is the sustain pedal. Knobs: drag up or down (shift for fine), arrow keys, double click to reset. A MIDI keyboard works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox after you press Connect MIDI. Safari has no Web MIDI, so on an iPhone or a Mac in Safari use the keys on screen.

MIDI files. Press rec and play: the first note starts the take, on whichever device the rail has lit. Play it back, or download it as a .mid. Import a .mid (or drop one on the strip) and pick Filament, Cathode or off for each track; both devices play at once. Render wav writes the file (or the take) through both devices and the rack, up to 10 minutes. Files stay in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Song library. Open songs and pick a tune, or drop a .mid file on that strip. Play and stop are the rack transport, and the tempo box (Rack tab) sets the speed. Each MIDI channel can go to Filament, Cathode, Dekatron, or off. Channel 10 starts on Dekatron. The bundled tunes are public domain, or original demos written for this page. Songs in the Halloween set load a matching Filament preset and a matching Cathode preset. Mountain King, Cellar Stairs and Porch Ghost also load a Dekatron pattern, and their hat part plays on Dekatron.

Privacy. No ads, no cookies, no accounts. Your patches and settings stay in this browser. DXEdge counts visits anonymously, the same way as the rest of the site. Tip the developer: helps cover hosting and development.
