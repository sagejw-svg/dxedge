SCULPT

Reach into a field of glowing orbs. Pinch one and move it. Up is pitch, across is pan, depth is the filter.

Rack Tip the developer

CAMERA

Sculpt uses your camera to see your hands. The picture stays in this browser. It is not recorded and it is not uploaded.

CALIBRATE

Hold your open hand in the ring, then pinch. That sets your reach and how tight a pinch grabs.

Allow the camera, or play with the mouse. Video is not uploaded.

How to play. Allow the camera, then pinch an orb between thumb and index. Move it left and right to pan, up and down for pitch, and closer or farther for the filter. Twist your wrist to brighten the tone. Spread the other fingers to explode it into grains. Open both hands and draw them apart to widen the reverb. Your hand is the skeleton in the scene.

Mouse. Drag to pinch and move. The wheel is depth. Alt drag twists. Shift drag, or [ ], spreads the fingers. Right-click plants the other hand; the gap widens the room. Q E twist. Arrow keys nudge.

Hand tracking is MediaPipe, running in this page. Apache 2.0. No server, and no video leaves the browser. Part of the Filament rack on DXEdge.
