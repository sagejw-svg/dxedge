Camera

Aether uses your camera to see both hands. The picture stays in this browser. It is not recorded or uploaded. You can play with the mouse instead, with no camera at all.

Calibrate

Move your right hand through the lowest, highest, quietest, and loudest places you want to play. Come close to the camera and back away. Pinch and spread the left hand, or move the sliders. Then press Done.

Choose the camera or the mouse.

Pitch runs left to right. Volume runs from quiet at the bottom to loud at the top. The lines on the picture are those zones.

How to play

  • Right hand, left to right: pitch. Up is louder, down is quiet. That is the volume.
  • Right hand closer to the camera opens the filter. Farther away darkens it.
  • Left-hand pinch adds vibrato. Spread fingers add reverb.
  • A fist, on either hand, mutes. The Mute button and the space bar do the same.
  • Snap to scale locks the pitch. Pick a scale and a root, or turn snap off for a continuous glide.
  • Smoothing steadies a shaky hand. Latency is how quickly the note glides to where your hand is.
  • Calibrate so the corners of the picture match your reach.
  • With the mouse: move in the picture for pitch and volume, use the wheel or the distance slider for the filter, and use the pinch and spread sliders for the left hand.
