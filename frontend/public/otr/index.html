Nothing here is an animation. The wax is simulated: the bulb heats the coil and the bottom of the glass, warm wax expands and rises, cools under the cap and sinks, necks stretch thin and pinch off, blobs that stay pressed together merge, and the liquid circulates around them, so it never repeats. Turn the bulb up and it runs hot (the wax stays up); turn it down and it gets lazy. Switch it off and the wax settles and sets. The first warm-up is shown at 3x. None of this changes the sound.
Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom (up to 8x), double-click to jump in or back out. + and − zoom, 0 fits, arrows move, Esc closes. Brightness, Response, Speed and Flicker change only the picture, never the sound. The filter also applies to the tube in the rack.
