## People

### Lewis Hamilton Smith: patentee of the pin (1926) and the adjacent SE1/4 (1920)

**Patents:** CASF 0010029, homestead, 1920-02-11, Sec 17 SE, 160 ac; and SER-979910, 1926-05-27, stock-raising homestead, 480 ac, minerals reserved. A homestead needs about 3-5 years' residence, so he likely lived on Sec 17 roughly 1915-1920 (**inference**).

**Not identified.** No Find a Grave memorial in California matches "Lewis Hamilton Smith." The Mississippi Lewis Hamilton Smith (1866-1961, Covington Co.) is a different man.

**Leads (guesses, not conclusions):**
1. The **Smith family of Pleyto/Bryson**: John Parks Hamilton Smith (b. 31 Mar 1848, Ohio; d. 4 Feb 1937, Pleyto; buried Pleyto Cemetery). He married Dora Thomas **Ray** Smith (1857-1957) of the pioneer Ray family. https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/21569798
   - Both use "Hamilton," and J.P.H. Smith's mother was Lucinda Martha **Hamilton**.
   - Lewis is **not** among J.P.H.'s five listed children: Viola Mary Nolan 1884-1968, Grace E. Birch 1885-1912, Hazel Gertrude Truitt 1887-1990, Sylvia Bell Miller 1892-1982, and Leland Herbert Smith 1895-1970.
   - J.P.H.'s brother was James Buchanan Smith (b. 1857). A nephew relationship is a **guess** only.
2. The **California Birth Index** lists a **Lewis Hamilton Smith born 21 Aug 1925 in San Luis Obispo County** (mother's maiden name Roth). Probable siblings are Beverly Odessa (1927, Los Angeles) and Marvin Lee (1935, Fresno). https://californiabirthindex.org/birth/beverly_odessa_smith_born_1927_1156374
   - The ranch is close to the San Luis Obispo County line. This child could be the homesteader's **son (guess)**, which would make the homesteader's wife née Roth (**guess**).
3. "Smith's Ranch" is printed on the 1919 and 1961 USGS topos near the pin. It may be this Smith's place or the J.P.H. Smith family's (**inference**).

**Next step:** the 1920 census for Bryson/San Antonio township. It is on FamilySearch, which needs a free login. Also Monterey County Recorder deeds after 1926.

### The Rogers patentees: Francis N., Nathan Jr., Nathan and Eliza F.

**Patents:**
- 1890: two **cash-entry** purchases in Sec 18, by Nathan Rogers Jr. and Francis N. Rogers.
- 1892: Nathan Rogers *et al.* homestead in Sec 20 (BLM lists Eliza F. Rogers with him).
- 1894: Francis N. Rogers **homestead** in Sec 17 (CACAAA 097694, Lockwood), the parcel just north-west of the pin: https://thelandpatents.com/patents/cacaaa-097694

A homestead means residence, so at least Francis lived here about 1889-1894 (**inference**).

**Name-only matches (low confidence; may be different people):**
- Francis N. Rogers, b. Oct 1872 in California, d. 1 Apr 1930 in San Francisco, buried Cypress Lawn, Colma. Wife Adele Hartshorne Rogers; daughter Adele Rogers Sharp (1898-1989). https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/255627666
  - He would have been only about 17 in 1889, which argues **against** this match.
- Nathan Rogers, b. 10 Oct 1870 in San Francisco, d. 2 Aug 1924, buried Cypress Lawn. His memorial notes a nearby crypt for an Eliza Rogers. https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/260639507
- The 1885 San Francisco directory lists a Dr. Nathan Rogers (physician), an Eliza Rogers (widow), and a Francis N. Rogers (printer). https://library.sfgenealogy.org/directory/san-francisco-county-directory/1885/1885_1005.pdf

A San Francisco family buying cash-entry land is plausible. The 1892 and 1894 homesteads, though, imply local residence. **Unresolved.**

### William Jasper Jarrett: 1883 cash sale (Sec 20) and 1893 homestead

- **Born** 1830 in Tennessee. **Died** 4 Jan 1904 at **Bryson**. **Buried** Pleyto Cemetery. https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/21520792
- **Origin:** son of John Jarrett Jr. (b. ~1792 Cabarrus Co., NC; d. 1838 Randolph Co., Arkansas) and Susana Russell (m. 1819).
  - Arkansas probate (1838-39) names him "William J. Jarrett," a minor heir living in Ripley County, Missouri, with guardian Jacob Wallace.
  - The family line goes back to the Plott family of Cabarrus County, NC.
  - Source: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/154797443
- **Spouse and children:** not found in free sources (unknown). A WikiTree William J. Jarrett (b. 1837, died Texas) is a **different** man.
- The 1893 homestead: https://thelandpatents.com/patents/cacaaa-097689
- **Confidence: high** for identity and burial; parents medium (contributor research on Find a Grave).

## Where these families are buried

**Pleyto Cemetery:** Jarrett, the Stanleys, the Meachams, and the J.P.H. Smith and Ray families.
- The old Pleyto townsite is under Lake San Antonio. The cemetery was relocated when the dam was built (1960s).
- Find a Grave: https://www.findagrave.com/cemetery/2231355/pleyto-cemetery

**King City Cemetery:** the Russells.
