Sky

Two unlisted photo histories, the Wilson family ranch and the Staggs family place in Ramona: aerial photos, satellite images and USGS maps, oldest first, with deep zoom, an overlay per image and a map of nearby historic sites.

Ranch SkyWilson family ranch, Sapaque / Waller Creek, Monterey County, CA. 18 images, 1919 to 2026. Ramona SkyStaggs family, 740 Progress Lane, Ramona, CA. 42 images, 1903 to 2026.

These pages are not linked from dxedge.net and carry noindex. The data, images and viewer code are kept in the public repo sagejw-svg/dxedge so the record outlasts its author.
